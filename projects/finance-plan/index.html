Summary

Get a clear cashflow overview.

A budget planner that focuses on planning ahead, not tracking past transactions. Users define recurring income and expenses, set budgets by week, month, or year, and get a clear cashflow overview at a glance through reports.

Features

What it does.

  • Accounts

    Creation of multiple accounts to organize transactions and split reports.

  • Planning Periods

    Weekly, monthly, and yearly budget planning in one app.

  • Recurring Transactions

    Bills and regular payments can be managed with minimal setup.

  • Category Organization

    Transactions can be organized using predefined categories and subcategories.

  • Accounts Summaries

    Summaries of income and expenses on accounts and across accounts.

  • Visual Reports

    Visualized reports transactions and price trends over time via bar and pie charts.

  • Category Reports

    Visualized reports spending in absolute and relative values based on categories and accounts.

  • Privacy first

    No collection of any kind of data.

  • Cloud Sync

    All calculations are synced with iCloud.
