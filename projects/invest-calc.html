Invest. Calc

iPhone · iPad · Launched May 2025

App Store

Simulate your financial future.

This app calculates investment growth over a specific timeframe using custom parameters like starting amount, contributions, expected growth, years to grow, and contribution growth. Contributions can be added in different intervals, e.g. monthly or yearly. The yearly balance is shown in a bar chart and list. Each past year can be assigned an achieved or missed status. All calculations are synced with iCloud.

Highlights.

  • Chart

    All calculated years are displayed in a color coded chart.

  • Year Details

    Many metrics of a year are available like annual contribution and interest.

  • Goal Tracking

    Each past year can be tracked with an achieved or missed status.

More features. 6 more things it does.

  • Contribution Frequency

    A contribution can be done weekly, biweekly, monthly, semiannually, or annually.

  • Contribution Growth

    A percentage of the contribution growth can be set to increase the contribution per year automatically.

  • Goals

    Goals are shown in the yearly list. These can also be toggled on or off.

  • Flexible Contributions

    The contribution for each year can be changed.

  • Privacy First

    No collection of any kind of data.

  • Cloud Sync

    All calculations are synced with iCloud.
