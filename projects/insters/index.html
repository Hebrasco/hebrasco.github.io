Summary

Color your memories.

Insters is a photo filter app. You can import photos from your photo library and choose from a variety of color filters to apply to your photo. If one of the filters is applied, you can export the photo back to your library.

Features

What it does.

  • Image Filters

    Various color filters can be applied to a photo.

  • Ads

    An ad banner is shown while the user is editing a photo.

  • In-App Purchases

    The ads can be removed through an in-app purchase.
