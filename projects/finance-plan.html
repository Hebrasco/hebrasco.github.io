Financial Plan

iPhone · iPad · Launched February 2026

App Store

Get a clear cashflow overview.

A budget planner that focuses on planning ahead, not tracking past transactions. Users set up their accounts, add recurring income and expenses from weekly to annually, and plan transfers between accounts. Every amount keeps a price history, so the reports show which costs went up, which changes are coming next and how each category develops over time. Income, expenses and surplus can be viewed per week, month, year or any other interval. Everything syncs through iCloud. The app is free to use, and a one-time purchase unlocks Premium.

Highlights.

  • Accounts

    Multiple accounts, each with its own income, expense and balance summary.

  • Price History

    Every amount keeps its history, and price changes can be scheduled ahead.

  • Price Changes

    A list of the costs that went up recently and the changes that are coming next.

More features. 9 more things it does.

  • Recurring Transactions

    Income and expenses that repeat weekly up to annually, with an optional end date.

  • Transfers

    Regular payments from one account to another, like a monthly amount moved to savings.

  • Categories

    Transactions are organized in predefined categories and subcategories.

  • Reports

    Income, expenses and surplus as charts, plus the savings rate.

  • Category Reports

    Spending per category and subcategory, across all accounts or per account.

  • Category Trends

    Bar charts that show how each category develops over time.

  • Privacy First

    No collection of any kind of data.

  • Cloud Sync

    All accounts and transactions are synced with iCloud.

  • Premium

    A one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited accounts, transfers and trends.
